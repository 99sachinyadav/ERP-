Goal Description
The objective is to introduce a leave request mechanism for staff alongside the existing teacher workflow. Additionally, we will empower the Director to assign leaves (EL, CL, ML, OD) from the director dashboard. CL and EL will be set per semester, whereas ML and OD will be configured yearly (academic year-wide).

Open Questions
IMPORTANT

Staff Department / HOD Routing: Since staff members do not have academic HODs, should their leave requests bypass HOD verification and be routed directly to the Director's queue (as status "FORWARDED_TO_DIRECTOR")? Our proposed plan automatically handles this bypass for staff.
Staff Login: Can staff members log in via the identical "Faculty Access" screen, which will automatically verify their Staff credentials and open the Staff Dashboard?
Verification Plan
Automated Tests
Postman or curl tests validating /api/registerStaff, /api/loginStaff, /api/leaves/director/assign.
Manual Verification
Log in as Director, assign leaves to classroom teachers, apply for a leave as a teacher, and verify the assigned leave balances are exact.
Log in as Admin, register a Staff member, log in as Staff, and verify they are redirected to a clean request-leave dashboard.
Log in as Director, assign leaves to Staff, log in as Staff, check balance and apply for a leave. Finally, verify the Director sees and approves it.
